| Piece | What it does here | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| **Next.js 15 App Router** | Pages and API in one app | Pages are built on the server straight from the database, so the browser downloads very little code (about 130 kB per page). |
| **Postgres (Neon) + Prisma** | Products, orders, carts | Prices and orders need a real relational database. Prisma keeps queries type-safe and handles migrations and the seed. Neon's free tier works well with Vercel. |
